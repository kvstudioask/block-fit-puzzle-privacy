Privacy Policy — Block Fit Puzzle

Last updated: October 24, 2026

This policy applies to Block Fit Puzzle for Android (package name: com.kvstudio.blockfit), published by KavuVeduStudio (“we”, “us”, or “our”).

Information stored by the game

The game stores progress, settings, and related gameplay data locally on your device. The game does not require you to create an account.

You can remove locally stored game data by clearing the app’s storage in Android settings or uninstalling the app.

Advertising and information handled by Google

The game uses the Google Mobile Ads SDK to show advertisements, including rewarded ads. Google’s SDK may automatically collect and share information such as:

  • IP address, which may be used to estimate general location;
  • Advertising or other device identifiers, where available;
  • Interactions with the app and ads, such as launches, taps, and video views;
  • Diagnostics and performance information, such as app launch time or errors.

Google and its advertising partners may use this information to provide and measure ads, understand ad interactions, improve services, and help detect fraud. Data handled by Google’s SDK is transmitted using encryption in transit. Google’s handling of this information is described in Google’s Privacy Policy and AdMob’s privacy information.

Retention and choices

Locally stored game data remains on your device until you clear the app’s storage or uninstall the game. Information collected by Google through its advertising services is handled and retained under Google’s policies and settings; uninstalling the game does not delete information already processed by Google.

You can manage advertising and privacy settings through Android device settings and Google’s ad settings.

Children

Block Fit Puzzle is intended for a general audience and is not designed specifically for children under 13. The game does not ask players to submit personal information directly. Advertising SDK data may still be handled as described in this policy. If you believe a child has provided personal information directly to us, contact us using the address below.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the game or its data practices change. We will update the date at the top when we publish a revision.
